How to play

Move with WASD/arrows. Aim & fire by holding on the arena, or hold F to fire toward the nearest enemy.

Dash with Space/Shift to move through danger. Pulse with Q to pierce up to four enemies. Release, then press again when ready.

Yellow diamonds show their charge direction before they rush. Step aside or dash across it, then attack while they recover. Health returns after a short respawn; restart to try for a cleaner run.

On touch screens, use the movement pad while holding the arena to aim and fire. Dash and Pulse are below it. Landscape gives you a larger view.

Connection help

Share the host's invite, then Start wave when everyone has joined. Keep the host's tab open. If joining fails, check the room code and try again. Multiplayer needs internet signaling; solo does not.
